Khadijah is your own voice assistant. She runs on this laptop only. You talk to her or type to her, and she answers in her own voice. Her answers are spoken, not written on the screen.

## What she can do today

The full list, with how to start each thing, is in **[What she can do](01a-what-she-can-do.md)**. In short:

- **Answer questions.** Type one and she replies. She can look things up on the web when she needs to.
- **Show you the laptop.** A strip across the top shows how hard the laptop is working.
- **Keep your tasks.** You can add a task, tick it off, change it, or delete it.
- **Show your calendar.** You can read your Google Calendar events, ask about them by voice, and add, change or delete them on the Calendar screen.
- **Answer short commands at once.** Switch AI, open a screen or a node by name, rotate the satellites (the logos on Home), and add, change or delete a task by voice. See **Spoken commands**.
- **Save notes.** Say "remember that" and what you want to keep, and she saves it.
- **Show your WhatsApp.** Read messages, reply by typing, see other languages in English, and read voice notes as words. See **WhatsApp**.
- **Show your money reports.** Read-only reports of Webull Main, Webull Crypto and Interactive Brokers, all three together, Claude Usage and the Thesis report. She never trades. You can also ask for your portfolio by voice. See **Webull reports**.
- **Show the news and the local cards.** Google News, the Cartagena weather, dollar and news, your Philly sports teams, and what is in theaters and coming soon. See their pages in the contents.
- **Send the morning brief.** At 0700 she sends a WhatsApp message with your day. See **Morning brief**.
- **Look after the laptop.** The Storage cleaner (only what you tick), the Network Mapper, the Bluetooth batteries, the weekly patch check, a look at your screen when you say yes, and the teardown of a web page. See **The machine and creative parts**.
- **Plan drone missions.** Plan a flight on a map for your DJI Mavic 4 Pro and save the flight file for the controller. Move the photos and videos into one folder for each mission. DaVinci Resolve Studio is its own part: it builds timelines from a footage folder and renders them.
- **Listen and speak.** The listening and speaking parts are built. Rafiq has tried her voice and the microphone and says both work. You can see her face move as she talks. That is her recorded face video; the separate live mouth that follows each word exactly is off on this laptop for now.

## What she cannot do yet

Every part is built. Some Mission Planner parts have not been tried yet. These are still open:

- The live mouth that follows each word exactly. It is off on this laptop.
- The 0700 morning brief arriving by itself. A test message works.

## Who can reach her

Only this laptop can reach her. Other computers and other web pages cannot.
