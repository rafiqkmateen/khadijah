# Khadijah guide: contents

Purpose: the one list of every page, so you can find what you need.
Owner: Rafiq. Version 0.2.1 (0.2.1: two page lines made complete, 2026-11-05; 0.2.0: agreed by Rafiq, 2026-11-04; 7 AM is now 0700; 0.1.0 agreed by Rafiq, 2026-11-02; the features page was added 2026-11-03, not yet agreed). Date: 2026-11-05.

The guide has two parts. Part A is for you. Part B is for whoever builds and repairs her. They are kept apart so the plain pages stay plain.

## Part A: The Guide

1. [Welcome](01-welcome.md): what she is, what she can do, what she cannot do yet.

1a. [What she can do](01a-what-she-can-do.md): everything she does today, how you start it and where it is explained.

2. [Start here](02-start-here.md): start her, ask a question, stop her.

3. [What you see on the screen](03-what-you-see.md): the menu, the stage, the strip, the panel, the bottom bar and the keys.
4. [Tasks and calendar](04-tasks-and-calendar.md): add, tick off, change and delete tasks; read your calendar.
5. [Your notes](05-notes.md): save a note and ask about your notes.

6. [Add, change or remove a key](06-keys.md): save the keys she needs.
7. [Change how times and dates are written](07-times-and-dates.md): follow Windows or choose your own.
8. [Settings you can change](08-settings.md): the settings in `config.json`, in plain words.
9. [Where she keeps things](09-where-she-keeps-things.md): her files and her log, and what you may delete.
10. [Keeping her private](10-keeping-her-private.md): what stops others reaching her.
11. [Words used here](11-words-used-here.md): the words this guide uses.
12. [Spoken commands](12-spoken-commands.md): the short phrases she answers at once.
13. [WhatsApp](13-whatsapp.md): connect your phone, read messages, reply, translation and voice notes.
14. [Webull reports](14-webull-reports.md): read your Webull, IBKR and combined reports, Claude Usage, Thesis + Pro's Read, and sign in once for the hidden window.
15. [Google News](15-google-news.md): read the top stories, your topics and what you follow.
16. [Cartagena News](16-cartagena-news.md): the weather, the dollar in pesos and local news for Cartagena.
17. [Philly sports](17-philly-sports.md): your teams' last and next games and their division tables.
18. [In Theaters and Coming Soon](18-the-cinema.md): what is playing at Plaza Bocagrande and what is coming.
19. [Morning brief](19-morning-brief.md): the 0700 WhatsApp message, what it holds, and how to send a test.
20. [The machine and creative parts](20-machine-and-creative-parts.md): Server Info, Storage, Network, batteries, patches, screen look, teardown, missions, Resolve, the Local LLM report and the Great Debate.
21. [Backups and housekeeping](21-backups-and-housekeeping.md): back up the project and watch for the random reboots.
22. Problems and fixes: start from what you see.
