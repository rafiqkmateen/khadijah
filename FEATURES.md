# What she does today

Purpose: every feature Khadijah has today, group by group. Only things that work are listed; nothing planned or switched off.
Owner: Rafiq. Counted from her guide on 2026-11-17: 78 features in 11 groups.

Designed and directed by Rafiq, built with Claude. Back to the [front page](README.md).

### Talk to her
- Answers a question in her own voice.
- Searches the web when a question needs current facts, and speaks a short summary.
- Answers fixed short phrases at once, without calling the AI, such as "which model", "which version" and "how many features do you have".
- Names five or six of the things she can do when you ask, a different handful each time, and says there is much more.
- Selects the AI model (Claude Sonnet, Claude Haiku or Google Gemini) and turns automatic routing on or off.
- Opens the Claude website or the Anthropic console in your browser.
- Opens a card or a place by voice.
- Speaks every reply in her own voice, OmniVoice.
- Makes a voice from a short recording, 3 to 25 seconds of any speaker.
- Speaks a cloned voice in any of the 646 languages, not only the language of the recording.
- Understands your speech in any language, with Whisper detecting the language from your voice.
- Says a word or sentence in another language when you ask how to say it, and speaks it back.
- Turns the microphone on or off, and stops a reply in the middle.
- Says one short line about a part of the screen when you open it.
- Suggests spellings and lets you add a word to the dictionary, in the message box and the WhatsApp reply box.

### Your own things
- Keeps your tasks: add, tick off, edit and delete, with an Undo bar on the Tasks screen.
- Asks what you mean when you start a task command without the details.
- Brings back a task you just deleted.
- Shows a Google Calendar as a month grid and lets you add, change and delete events with an event form.
- Answers questions about your calendar for any dates up to a year ahead.
- Saves a note, and answers questions from your notes.
- Writes times and dates the way you choose.

### WhatsApp
- Shows an incoming message in its chat on the WhatsApp screen, silently.
- Holds up to ten chats at once.
- Sends your typed reply to the open chat only.
- Shows a message that is not in English in English.
- Sends your reply in the contact's language while the chat still shows your English.
- Shows the language your replies go out in, in the chat header (for example "Replies: Spanish (auto)").
- Saves your own name for a contact.
- Writes an incoming voice note out as words on this laptop and shows it in English as "[Voice note]" followed by the words.
- Connects to your phone once, and restarts its own connection if it stops.
- Sends you the morning brief at 0700, or when she starts before 1100 if the laptop was off at 0700.
- Sends the morning brief once on request as a test, marked "(test send)".

### Money reports
- Shows a read-only report from your brokerage account.
- Shows your stock balances and positions from a second brokerage, read-only.
- Shows your crypto balances, read-only.
- Shows every account in one report, and for a longer time frame by voice. She never places a trade.
- Shows your Claude plan limits and spending.
- Closes every Claude window and opens the app again to free its memory.
- Reads your accounts, balances, positions and orders herself, hands them to a Claude project, runs it and shows the report.

### News, sports and the movies
- Shows the top stories, your topics and what you follow.
- Shows Cartagena's weather, the dollar in pesos and local news.
- Shows your favorite teams' last and next games and their tables.
- Shows what is playing at a local cinema.
- Shows the films that are coming soon.

### The laptop
- Shows a live view of what the server is doing.
- Shows the disk, and cleans only what you tick, asking twice first.
- Shows every device on your Wi-Fi and flags the ones it does not know with a red dot.
- Checks your wired or Wi-Fi connection and says in plain words what is wrong and what to check first.
- Lists the background programs she would close and the memory that frees, then closes them after you say go.
- Opens Khadijah's own page on GitHub in a new browser tab, straight away.
- Checks for security updates each week, installs the safe ones herself after her tests pass, puts everything back if one fails, and tells you on the screen and aloud.
- Looks at your screen and describes it, only after you say yes.
- Reads a website, a program or an app.
- Watches the files in her folder and logs every change.
- Makes a dated backup of the project.

### Drone, video and ideas
- Plans a drone flight on a map and saves it as a file for the drone.
- Builds and renders a timeline of your footage in DaVinci Resolve Studio.
- Has two voices argue your plan until they agree.
- Shows a written report about running an AI model on this laptop, with a source link on each fact, and looks for new items on eight public sites when you press Check for new items.

### Stop, restart and connection
- Stops Khadijah completely.
- Restarts Khadijah.
- Keeps one live page.
- Reconnects by itself when the link to her server drops.

### Warnings
- Turns the glow round the edges of the screen red when the link to her server is lost, or for a few seconds when a reply fails.
- Turns a number in the strip across the top amber and then red when it stays high.
- Shows six readings in the strip across the top: processor, memory, graphics, graphics memory, graphics temperature and free space on drive C.
- Puts a red ring round her face, with a short red label under her pill (for example KEYBOARD 20%), when a Bluetooth device's battery is at 20 percent or lower.

### Safety
- Lets only this laptop reach her.
- Never sends a WhatsApp message on her own.

### The screen
- Shows her screen, The City: real footage of a place for each part of her, which follows the time of day by itself, with the menu, the strip across the top with the date and clock, her pill, the event line and the message bar on top.
- Shows every card as a name on Home, over Philadelphia, in eight colored groups.
- Shows a place on its own footage, with its cards listed at the right.
- Closes a card with the X, with Escape, or by itself after a minute (longer for the money reports, the cinema, Network Doctor and the Great Debate).
- Shows her pill with her face, her name, her state (STANDBY, LISTENING, THINKING or TALKING) and the AI in use.
- Lists the newest four events with their times on the event line at the lower left, such as "Mic on" or "Tasks updated".
- Keeps a soft calm glow round the edge of the screen, dimmer while the microphone is off, and turns it red as a warning.
- Lets you change the keys she needs, and the settings in her file.
